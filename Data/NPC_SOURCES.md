# Service NPC data sources

`ServiceNPCs.lua` contains a reduced and transformed selection of service NPC and trainer records from [Waypoint Tracker](https://github.com/sanjaygbhat/wowforever-waypoint-tracker), commit `28cc7513a23a5abf9fad27be5fbb4fe5a8b0156b` (copyright 2026 sanjaygbhat, MIT). Its MIT license is in `WaypointTracker-LICENSE.txt`.

The selection combines its service roster, curated positions, shared Forever service observations, and travel-network inn locations, retaining only IDs, names, faction access, map IDs, and positions. Missing inn locations are filled from the MIT-licensed Mapzeroth travel data bundled by Waypoint Tracker; see `WaypointTracker-TRAVEL-LICENSES.txt`. Trainer records combine the upstream class and profession rosters and observed positions, with curated positions filling gaps. Repair, banker, auctioneer, and stable master records combine service rosters with positioned records. Reagent vendors are identified from explicit titles or merchants listed as selling both Arcane Powder and Infernal Stones; their present stock is not guaranteed. Observations override curated positions where available. For rostered NPCs still missing a position, the importer can use Classic spawn coordinates when the old area name uniquely matches a Forever zone map in Blizzard client tables (build 1.60.1.70862). It skips Mulgore, Eastern Plaguelands, Redridge Mountains, and Stormwind because those maps were reshaped. Classic-derived coordinates are marked `Classic` and are not verified in Forever. Each generated record includes a source label: `Observed`, `Community`, `Curated`, `Travel`, or `Classic`; these describe the source, not a guarantee that the NPC is present or accessible. The source bundles data and observations from All The Things, pfQuest, Efficient Games and other sources; attribution and license notices are preserved in `WaypointTracker-THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`. In particular, Forever service observations from Efficient Games are licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and have been selected and reformatted here. Game content remains the property of Blizzard Entertainment.

To regenerate, check out the pinned upstream commit and run `node Tools/extract-service-npcs.js <path-to-WaypointTracker_Data> Data/ServiceNPCs.lua` from the project root. The script prints the number of rostered NPCs with positions for each supported service. These totals measure only the upstream rosters, not all NPCs in the game. The selected data is incomplete; a missing record does not prove that a zone has no service NPC. Client patches may also change faction access or NPC positions.
