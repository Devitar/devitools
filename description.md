## A suite of quality-of-life tools for World of Warcraft: Forever

Devitools helps you keep track of buffs, find useful NPCs, and make space in your bags. Each tool can be enabled or disabled independently for each character, and the buff and locator windows can be moved to suit your UI.

## Buff Reminder

Keep an eye on your buffs with a movable reminder window. Devitools reminds you when a supported buff you've learned is missing or about to expire. Missing buffs appear in red and expiring buffs appear in yellow.

- Choose which buffs you want to track and how far in advance you want to be warned. The default warning time is five minutes.
- Optionally track **Well Fed** and the **Well-Rested** buff from the Cozy Sleeping Bag; both are off by default.
- Enable situational reminders, such as Detect Invisibility and Unending Breath, separately. These default off.
- Track Hunter aspects and Trueshot Aura, Paladin auras, and Shaman elemental shields. Righteous Fury and preferred Druid forms or Warrior stances are optional and off by default.
- Track Demonic Sacrifice as one reminder for any of its four warlock effects.
- Track temporary weapon coatings, including sharpening stones, weightstones, and oils, as well as class-specific poisons, shaman imbues, and warlock Firestones or Spellstones. These reminders are optional and off by default.
- Add an active buff, such as an elixir, to your manually tracked buffs. You can toggle or remove each one in settings, and your list is saved separately for each character.
- Right-drag the reminder window to move it, or lock it in place. Click its cogwheel to open Buff Reminder settings directly. The window hides during combat and when there are no reminders to show.

The current list covers common buffs for druids, hunters, mages, paladins, priests, shamans, warlocks, and warriors. It is a starting point rather than a complete list of every class buff.

## Where is...

Looking for an innkeeper or a trainer? Click the movable **Where is...** button and choose a service to find a nearby NPC available to your faction.

- Find innkeepers, flight masters, repair vendors, bankers, auctioneers, stable masters, reagent vendors, your class trainer, and profession trainers.
- Place a map pin and see a walking route on your minimap and world map when one is available. No separate navigation addon is required.
- If none are known in your current zone, Devitools reports the nearest known match on your continent and pins it.
- Clear the pin manually, or let Devitools clear it when you arrive near the recorded destination.
- Customize which services and map routes appear, the arrival distance, chat messages, and search options. Right-drag the button to move it, or lock it in place.

NPC locations and walking maps are not complete and may become outdated, so a result or route may sometimes be missing or inaccurate.

## Bag Cleaner

Need to make room? Click **Make space** above the bag window, enter how many slots you want to free, and review the lowest vendor-value items. Hover over an item to see its tooltip, or right-click to ignore that suggestion or add it to your exclusion list. You can confirm deletion of one item, or confirm a list and delete its items one click at a time. Deletion removes the full quantity in a bag slot. Quest items, locked slots, Hearthstones, Soul Shards, ammunition, and conjured items are excluded; add more items by name in settings. You can also set a default quantity or filter for poor-quality or sellable items. Each slot is checked again before deletion. Vendor value does not reflect auction value or how useful an item might be to you.

## Settings

Type **/dvt** or open **Game Menu > Options > AddOns > Devitools**. Expand Devitools in the settings sidebar to find a separate page for each tool.

More tools may be added over time. Suggestions are welcome!
